Common Questions

FAQ

Do you offer custom furniture designs?

Yes — every piece is made-to-order. Our design consultants work with you to understand your space, style and budget, then craft a unique piece tailored to your exact requirements.

How long does it take to deliver?

Standard pieces typically take 3–4 weeks. Fully custom designs may take 6–8 weeks depending on complexity. We provide assembly and professional installation as part of our delivery service.

What materials do you use?

We use premium solid woods including teak, sheesham, and engineered hardwoods, complemented by high-quality hardware and fabrics. All materials are ethically sourced and selected for longevity.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every piece of furniture comes with a 5-year craftsmanship warranty. This covers structural integrity, joinery, and hardware. We stand behind every item we make.

Can I visit your showroom?

Absolutely. Our showroom is located at D-35/76 Jangamwadi, Varanasi. We're open Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 7 PM. Walk in or call ahead to arrange a design consultation at no charge.
