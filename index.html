Our Story

Where Craft
Meets Character

Founded on the belief that every home deserves furniture that reflects its owner's soul, International Furnishers has been creating heirloom-quality pieces since 1998. Each item is hand-selected, master-crafted, and designed to endure — not just trends, but generations.

From our workshop in Lucknow to living rooms across India, we bring together time-honoured joinery techniques and contemporary sensibility.
